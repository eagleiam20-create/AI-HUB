How AI HUB works

AI HUB helps you discover AI services and organise your own productivity workflow. It does not own or include the third-party AI models.

↗ Official AI websites

When you open Gemini, Claude, ChatGPT or another service, you leave AI HUB and use that provider's website and account. Their rules and usage limits apply.

◈ Credits in this prototype

Credits currently demonstrate how a wallet could look. They do not track third-party usage and have no monetary value in this demo.

🔐 Privacy first

This prototype stores saved tools and demo credits in your browser only. Don't use it as a production account or payment system.

🚀 What comes next?

A production launch needs a secure backend, account system, payment verification, privacy/terms pages, and a tested reason for customers to pay.
